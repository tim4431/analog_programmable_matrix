4 x 4 ANALOG MATRIX - PCB D / ELECTRICAL A2

FABRICATION
Board: 160.00 x 170.00 mm, four copper layers, nominal 1.60 mm FR-4.
Recommended copper: 35 um on all four layers; Tg >= 150 C; ENIG finish.
Nominal dielectric stack: 0.20 mm / 1.06 mm / 0.20 mm. The fabricator may
adjust this to its available 1.6 mm stack; confirm before fabrication.
L1: components, short analog feedback paths, local bypass, other signals.
L2: GND plane with small copper windows at fast-amplifier signal pins.
L3: supply distribution and secondary signal routing.
L4: remaining routing and stitched ground copper.
Minimum permitted copper clearance/trace: 0.15 mm / 0.15 mm.
Signal routing: 0.15/0.20 mm. Main supply trunks: 0.60 mm; local supply
branches: 0.20/0.30 mm. Fine-pitch pad escapes may neck down to 0.15 mm.
Normal vias: 0.60 mm copper / 0.30 mm finished hole.
Fine-pitch signal escapes: 0.50 mm copper / 0.25 mm finished hole.
Thermal vias: 0.50 mm copper / 0.20 mm finished hole (THS3091 footprint
also includes 0.20 mm thermal holes). Minimum annular ring: 0.10 mm.
Six 3.20 mm unplated M3 mounting holes. Use insulating spacers/washers;
the holes provide mechanical mounting, not a chassis-ground terminal.
Hole centers in mm from top-left: (4,4), (156,4), (4,126), (156,126),
(4,166), (156,150). The lower-right hole is offset to clear its heatsink.
Use the supplied Excellon PTH and NPTH files separately. Do not merge
mounting holes into plated electrical holes.
Fill and cap all via-in-pad holes, including those beneath ADA4899 and
THS3091 exposed pads and signal escapes within SMD lands, or agree an
assembly-qualified anti-wicking process with the fabricator/assembler.
See via_in_pad_locations.csv for the conservative via-in-pad location map.
Ordinary open holes within solder-mask openings can drain solder paste.
Tent ordinary signal/ground vias. Exposed pads MUST be soldered to GND.
The Gerbers contain the nominal outline and all four copper layers.
No controlled-impedance fabrication requirement is claimed for this board.

ASSEMBLY
All components are on the front. The placement file includes through-hole
parts; exclude those from an SMT-only assembly job. Four input termination
resistors are intentionally DNP: the inputs are high impedance by default.
The BOM specifies IC ordering codes and passive values, packages and
ratings. Select actual purchasable passive MPNs before placing an assembly
order; it is not a turnkey procurement BOM.
Output source termination: 49.9 ohm, 2512, >= 1 W, each channel.
At 4 V DC into a 50-ohm load, its dissipation is approximately 0.32 W.
Use original Raspberry Pi Pico with two matching 20-position sockets.
J1-J8: Amphenol 031-5329-52RFX vertical 50-ohm BNC, hand soldered.
The local footprint uses the Amphenol catalog p118 Fig2 / pattern F:
6.90 mm square ground-pin pitch, 2.40 mm ground drills, 1.30 mm center drill.
Do not substitute the former SMA connector or a generic BNC footprint.
J9 now faces outward at the right edge. Pin 1 +15 V: (155,143), pin 2 GND:
(155,137.92), pin 3 -15 V: (155,132.84), in PCB coordinates. Check polarity
by pin number; its visual order has changed because the connector rotated.
AD5544BRSZ is SSOP-28, not TSSOP-28. ADA4899-1YRDZ uses the supplied custom
2.29 x 2.29 mm exposed-pad footprint. Do not substitute a generic SOIC.

REGULATOR COOLING AND MECHANICS
Fit heatsinks on both L7805CV and L7905CV. Budget 4 W per regulator for
initial mechanical design. This is a design allowance, not a measured load.
M3 reserves two 35 x 13 x 38.1 mm sink envelopes, each starting 0.5 mm
above the PCB. See the M3 thermal calculation and first-article acceptance.
The reduced PCB/enclosure area requires renewed full-load thermal testing.
The front-end ICs alone draw about 294 mA from EACH 5 V rail at idle;
the 15-to-5 V regulators therefore dissipate roughly 3 W EACH at idle.
4 W is a design allowance, not a verified maximum. Measure loaded currents.
At 35 C room ambient, 4 W with an effective sink-to-room resistance of
14 C/W, 2 C/W interface and 5 C/W junction-case predicts Tj=119 C.
Require that resistance or better WITH THE ENCLOSURE FITTED and both rails
loaded; a catalog free-air number does not establish enclosure performance.
Heatsink bases must remain <=91 C at that 4 W / 35 C acceptance point.
Reject the smaller sink if junction estimate exceeds 120 C, either rail
dissipation exceeds 4 W, or enclosure material temperatures exceed rating.
Mounting-plane CAD Y=13.54; PCB X centers 102.46 and 140.46 mm.
Select bolted sinks that fit the envelope without incompatible locating pins.
The included fin shapes are clearance models, not qualified thermal parts.
The M3 3 mm G10 bridge uses two 50 mm M3 posts and compliant top pads.
No new heatsink locating-pin holes have been added to the PCB.
L7805 tab is GND. L7905 tab is -15 V. Use separate sinks or insulating
mounting hardware; never bolt both bare tabs to the same conductive sink.
The THS3091 drivers dissipate roughly 0.85 W each at the worst full-scale
DC output under nominal supplies, including approximately 0.285 W idle.
Their soldered PowerPADs and ground-plane thermal connections are essential.

POWER AND FIRST CHECKS
1. Inspect fine-pitch ICs, exposed pads, diode bands and capacitor polarity.
   Check each supply to GND for accidental shorts before applying power.
2. Connect a current-limited, regulated +/-15 V supply to J9, following the
   +15V / GND / -15V markings. Allow roughly 1 A available per rail for the
   complete board; determine the final current limits from measurements.
3. Apply analog supplies before USB power to the Pico. Verify +5A=+5 V and
   -5A=-5 V. These are voltage-net names, not five-ampere current ratings.
   The design has no analog-supply supervisor; retain this power sequence.
4. Load the included MicroPython firmware on the original Pico. Firmware
   starts with output mute asserted. See firmware/CONTROL.txt for commands.
5. With external 50-ohm terminations fitted, check DC offsets at zero matrix,
   then test one coefficient/path at a time using a small low-frequency input.
6. Calibrate both coefficient signs and row DC offsets. Factory coefficient
   scale 1.021 is an estimate. Replace it with measured calibration values.
7. Verify gain flatness to 1 MHz, step ringing/settling, channel crosstalk,
   output loading, and regulator/driver temperatures before full-scale use.

OPERATING CONVENTIONS
Inputs: +/-1 V peak, DC coupled. Outputs: +/-4 V peak INTO EXTERNAL 50 OHMS.
The onboard 49.9-ohm resistor is a series source termination. A high-
impedance oscilloscope input sees nearly twice the specified loaded output.
Digital magnitude/sign controls set static analog gains; the Pico does not
sample the signal. Keep the digital interface idle during sensitive captures.
The inherited A2 SPICE model supports the circuit choice, but is NOT an
extracted PCB simulation or a hardware measurement. PCB parasitics and
thermal/assembly behavior still require prototype validation. Small-
coefficient relative error is not a design acceptance target for this build.
