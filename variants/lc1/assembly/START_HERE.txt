LC1 ASSEMBLY DATA - SEPARATE FROM REVISION D

Use only with the LC1 PCB and LC1 firmware. The original JLCPCB order has
not been changed. Catalog part identities are not reserved assembly stock.

365 fitted SMT components, 35 grouped BOM rows, all on top.
18 through-hole/module items are in MANUAL_ASSEMBLY.csv.
4 resistors are intentionally DNP and excluded from BOM/CPL.
Additional Pico sockets/headers, heatsinks, tab insulators and enclosure
hardware are separate. See the original M3 mechanical instructions.

Read ../SOURCING.md before quoting. OPA4197IPWR catalog stock was only one
piece when checked, insufficient for two boards. OPA2810IDR JLC availability
and price remain unconfirmed. Blank LCSC cells need exact part selection.
Do not silently replace TI amplifiers with another manufacturer or family.

Coordinates preserve native board origins: X rightward, Y negative downward
from the PCB top-left. KiCad CCW rotation is normalized to 0..359 degrees.
JLC library zero-angle conventions may differ; inspect every IC pin 1,
polarized capacitor and connector against PIN1_ORIENTATION_REFERENCE.csv
and the native PCB/assembly drawing. No vendor rotation offsets are guessed.

Analog resistors: preserve the listed precision and temperature coefficient.
TIA feedback capacitors: 1.0 pF C0G +/-0.1 pF; these differ from Revision D.
Output source resistors: 49.9 ohm, 0.1%, at least 1 W, 2512.
The MCP4728 must have default I2C address 0x60.

PCB target: 160 x 170 mm, 4 layers, 1.6 mm nominal FR-4, Tg >=150 C,
35 um copper on all four layers or explicit CAM confirmation, ENIG.
L1 signals, L2 ground, L3 signals/power, L4 signals/ground.
Filled and copper-capped thermal/via-in-pad holes are still required.
Use POFV service, not ordinary tenting/plugging. Obtain CAM acceptance for
small thermal holes under exposed-pad amplifiers. Do not fill component
lead holes or nonplated mounting holes. No assembly order is authorized by
a catalog identity match or a successful design-rule check alone.

Qualification: unbuilt prototype. Complete rail/offset checks, individual
positive/negative coefficient calibration with a 50 ohm load, 1 MHz response,
full-sum swing, oscillation checks and enclosure thermal soak. Apply analog
power before USB. Install insulated regulator heatsinks before operation;
the negative regulator tab is not ground. See the simulation review PDF and
LC1 firmware/CONTROL.md for calibration and model limitations.
